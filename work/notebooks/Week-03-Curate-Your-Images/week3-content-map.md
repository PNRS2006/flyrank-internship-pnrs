# 7. Evidence Rules

The portfolio will prioritize real evidence over decorative content.

### For completed projects

Use:

- Real screenshots
- Real GitHub repositories
- Real live demos
- Real dashboards
- Real results

### For unfinished projects

Clearly label them as:

- In Progress
- Research
- Upcoming

### For internship work

Only publish material that is publicly shareable and does not expose confidential information.

### What will not be fabricated

I will not invent:

- Performance metrics
- User counts
- Business impact
- ML accuracy
- Testimonials
- Production deployment
- Dataset results
- Client/company claims
- Internship results
