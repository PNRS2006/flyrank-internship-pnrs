I rejected the AI-generated futuristic developer workspace because
it looked visually attractive but communicated a generic coding/AI
aesthetic rather than evidence of my actual work. A real project
screenshot provides stronger proof and is more aligned with my
portfolio's credibility-first direction.

## Where I Chose Real Over AI

I chose real screenshots for my projects because they demonstrate
actual work I built. I chose a real photograph for my personal
identity rather than an AI-generated portrait. AI-generated imagery
is only used for limited decorative elements where it does not
misrepresent my work.
